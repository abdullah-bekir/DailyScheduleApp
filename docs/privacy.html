Planly Privacy Policy

App: Planly (DailyscheduleApp) · Bundle ID: com.abdullahbekir.DailyscheduleApp · Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

Planly helps you manage daily tasks and plans. We respect your privacy and collect only what is needed to run the app.

Data we collect

  • Task and app data: Tasks, completion state, and preferences (theme, language, etc.) are stored on your device.
  • Cloud sync: When Supabase is configured, you may create an account with a username and password. Tasks and settings can sync to our database for signed-in users.
  • Advertising: The free version may use Google AdMob and related identifiers to show ads.
  • Subscriptions: Planly Pro purchases are handled by the Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We do not collect your payment card details.
  • Diagnostics: Limited technical logs (e.g. crashes) may be collected to improve stability.

How we use data

To provide features, optional sync, ads, subscriptions, and to fix issues. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers (Supabase, Google/AdMob, RevenueCat, and store platforms) as needed to operate the app.

Retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. For cloud data: open Settings → Data management → Reset all data or Delete cloud account. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children

Planly is not directed at children under 13.

Terms and Conditions

By creating a Planly account you agree to use the app lawfully, keep your password private, and accept that task data may sync via our cloud service when enabled. Subscriptions follow Apple App Store or Google Play rules. We may update these terms; continued use means acceptance of the current version.

Gizlilik Politikası (TR)

Genel

Planly günlük görev ve planlarınızı yönetmenize yardımcı olur. Gizliliğinize saygı duyarız.

Toplanan veriler

  • Görev ve uygulama verileri cihazınızda saklanır.
  • Supabase yapılandırıldığında anonim hesap ile bulut senkronu kullanılabilir; e-posta ile giriş zorunlu değildir.
  • Ücretsiz sürümde AdMob reklamları için ilgili tanımlayıcılar kullanılabilir.
  • Abonelikler mağaza ve RevenueCat üzerinden işlenir; ödeme kartı bilgilerinizi toplamayız.

Saklama ve silme

Yerel veriler uygulama kaldırılınca silinir. Bulut verisi için: Ayarlar → Veri yönetimi → Tüm veriyi sıfırla veya Bulut hesabını sil. E-posta ile de talep edebilirsiniz.
